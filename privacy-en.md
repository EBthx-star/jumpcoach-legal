## In one sentence

JumpCoach **does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data**. All your course walks, horse profiles, and settings stay **on your iPhone**.

---

## What JumpCoach **does not** do

- ❌ No user accounts, no sign-up
- ❌ No server, no JumpCoach backend
- ❌ No analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
- ❌ No advertising identifier (IDFA), no ads
- ❌ No sharing with third parties
- ❌ No cross-app tracking (App Tracking Transparency: declined by default)

---

## Data JumpCoach uses, **locally on your device**

### GPS location
- **Why:** trace your course walk and geolocate obstacles.
- **Where:** app memory only, inside JumpCoach's container.
- **Shared with Apple?** No.

### Microphone + speech recognition
- **Why:** identify the voice commands "Vertical", "Oxer", "Palanque" you dictate to drop an obstacle hands-free.
- **Where:** processing is done by **Apple's speech recognition**. Depending on your iPhone settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Dictation / Siri), audio may be transmitted to Apple's servers for transcription. **JumpCoach only receives the final text** and does not store the audio.
- **Apple documentation:** [https://support.apple.com/HT210657](https://support.apple.com/HT210657)

### Motion (accelerometer)
- **Why:** measure your horse's stride length and detect jumps during ride mode.
- **Where:** local processing, no transmission.

### Course walks and horse profiles
- **Where:** stored in `UserDefaults` (standard iOS preferences), inside JumpCoach's private container.
- **Backups:** at your request, JumpCoach generates a `.json` file you can share to **iCloud Drive** via the iOS share sheet. You decide where to send it; Apple applies its own iCloud privacy policy to that storage.

---

## Apple Watch ↔ iPhone communication

If you use the Apple Watch companion app, obstacles dropped from the wrist are sent to your iPhone over **WatchConnectivity** (Apple's private channel between your two paired devices). No data leaves your devices.

---

## iOS permissions requested

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| **Location while in use** | Record the GPS track during the on-foot course walk |
| **Location in background** | Keep recording while the iPhone is in your pocket during the ride |
| **Microphone** | Dictate voice commands to drop obstacles |
| **Speech recognition** | Translate dictation into commands |
| **Motion (accelerometer)** | Measure stride length and detect jumps |

You can revoke any permission at any time in **Settings → JumpCoach**.

---

## Your rights (GDPR)

Because **no data ever leaves your iPhone**, there is nothing to export, rectify, or delete on the publisher's side. The data is yours and stays with you.

To delete all your JumpCoach data: **delete the app**. Everything is erased.
